Study Title: Decision-Making in Online Negotiations
Researcher: Enock Wanderema, MSc Behavioural Science

NOTE:

Before you decide whether to participate, it is important that you understand why the research is being conducted and what it will involve. Please read the following information carefully.

Purpose of the study

This study investigates how people make decisions and behave in online negotiations.

Why you?
{{ if is_prolific }}

You have been invited because you are a Prolific participant aged 18–65, based in the United Kingdom, and fluent in English. We are recruiting approximately 150–200 participants for this study.

{{ elif is_volunteer }}

You have been invited to help pilot this study before it is launched to a wider participant pool.

{{ else }}

You have been invited because you are aged 18–65 and fluent in English.

{{ endif }}
Participation terms

Participation is entirely voluntary. You are free to withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any consequences.

What you will do

The study has four short parts:

  1. Read a brief scenario about a business negotiation between two parties (2 minutes)
  2. Answer a few questions about your expectations before the negotiation (2 minutes)
  3. Negotiate an equity split with a partner via a real-time text chat interface (5–7 minutes)
  4. Complete a short post-negotiation survey about your experience (3 minutes)

Estimated total duration: 12–15 minutes.

Any risks?

This study presents no risks beyond those encountered in everyday life. The negotiation scenario is fictional and involves no real business decisions.
